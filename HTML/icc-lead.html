Indiana Cancer Consortium (ICC) Website Redesign [UX Experience Studio School Project]

Role

UX/UI Design Lead, Communication Lead (January 2026 - May 2026)

Skills / programs

  • Figma
  • Figma Make AI
  • UX Research

Summary

The ICC is a member-based organization that works to bring together individuals and organizations working in the cancer space in Indiana, and the ICC website is essential in displaying key resources for their members and guests. However, the current website has experienced no major changes since its inception 10 years ago, and currently has organizational, navigational, and monochromacity issues. As the UX/UI design lead, I designed the interactive Figma prototype, leveraging Figma Make AI in the process, and conducted current-state and redesign tests for the ICC website to assess the efficiency of our prototype. I also acted as the communications lead for the team, regularly coordinating meetings and updating project sponsors on progress.

Outcomes

  • High-Fidelity interactive Figma prototype; reduced information search time by 73% compared to the original site
  • Design documentation
