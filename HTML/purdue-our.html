Purdue Office of Undergraduate Research (OUR) Website Redesign [UX Experience Studio School Project]

Role

UX Researcher, UX Designer (January 2025 - May 2025)

Skills / programs

  • Figma
  • User Flow
  • UX Research

Summary

The Purdue OUR is responsible for informing students about potential undergraduate research opportunities and providing resources for students to participate in research projects across a variety of different departments. However, students have expressed frustration and difficulty with navigating the current website, making it underutilized. I assisted the creation of the final annotated user flow and interactive high-fidelity prototype of our redesign on Figma. Some of our recommendations have actually been implemented by the Purdue OUR on their current website.

Outcomes

  • High-Fidelity interactive Figma prototype; some changes were actually implemented on the current OUR website.
    • Search Opportunities
    • Undergraduate Research Programs
  • Annotated user flow
  • Design documentation
